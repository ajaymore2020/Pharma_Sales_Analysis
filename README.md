🔍 Overview
The Pharmaceutical Sales Analysis Dashboard is a comprehensive business intelligence solution built in Power BI to help stakeholders monitor, analyze, and act on pharmaceutical sales data. It covers performance across multiple dimensions — time, geography, sales channel, product class, and team — enabling strategic decision-making and performance benchmarking.
📊 Key Metrics
MetricValue💰 Gross Sales$9.14 Billion👥 Total Customers203K📦 Quantity Sold22 Million Units🧪 Total Products240

✨ Features

📅 Time-range slider — filter data between 2022 and 2025
🏥 Channel toggle — switch between Hospital and Pharmacy views
🏆 Best Performance panel — highlights top Manager, Team, and Salesperson
📈 Yearly Sales trend — line/area chart tracking annual revenue
🍩 Sales by Team — donut chart breakdown across Delta, Charlie, Bravo, and Alfa teams
📊 Quantity by Sub-channel — horizontal bar chart (Retail, Government, Institution, Private)
🗺️ Quantity by Manager — treemap view for quick manager-level comparison
🎯 Gross Sales gauge — radial gauge showing performance against target
🔖 Product Class filters — checkboxes for Analgesics, Antibiotics, Antimalarial, Antipiretics, Antiseptics, Mood Stabilizers


📄 Dashboard Pages
1. Overall Performance Analysis — Key Insights
A narrative summary page presenting the major takeaways from the data, suitable for executive stakeholders who need a high-level story without raw charts.
Highlights include:

Total gross sales and unit volume
Best-performing team and manager identification
Year-over-year performance trends
Top-contributing channels and product categories
Business impact summary

2. Overall Performance Analysis — Visual Analytics
The full interactive analytics page with all charts and filters, designed for analysts and sales managers for deep-dive exploration.
